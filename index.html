Every Coke opens a story.

A weekly family question for Filipino Gen Z newcomers. Buying a glass Coke 2-pack unlocks it, Lola answers by voice, and the story is kept in the family's tapestry.

The AppStep 1 of 9

Monday: a new question arrives

Every Monday the same question goes to every family. It stays locked until someone buys a glass Coke 2-pack.

How the recommendation works

Three parts, one weekly habit. The 2-pack creates the moment, the app makes it worth repeating, and the community brings Gen Z in.

  1. 01

    The Pack

    Creates the moment

    A glass Coke 2-pack: one for you, one for Lola. A shelf card beside it points to the app. The pack itself doesn't change.

    37%of Coke in the Philippines is sold in glass bottles
  2. 02

    The App

    Makes it worth repeating

    A new question every Monday, written by Filipino advisors. Lola answers by voice; it's transcribed, translated, and framed by a Filipino artist.

    ~66 daysfor a weekly habit to become automatic
  3. 03

    The Community

    Brings Gen Z in

    Stories become short subtitled clips that families post on TikTok and Reels. Launch in Winnipeg with student associations, churches and settlement agencies.

    1 in 10people in Winnipeg is Filipino
